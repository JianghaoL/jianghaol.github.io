Overview

An instrumental piece produced in Logic Pro.

With a 2-week production duration, Hanyu Qin and I composed the music, recorded many different instruments, and eventually mixed them.

Recording

With a rather limited recording environment, we made an effort to record the drumset, saxophone, and percussion.
