About the Redesign

Ripout is a science-fiction game featuring first-person shooter elements. This sound redesign creates a fresh sonic experience for the game's combat sequences, environmental sounds, and UI effects.

The clip discards all of its original audio. Every sound was rebuilt from scratch using the Vital synthesizer, combined with Foley, commercial sound-library editing, and mixing techniques.

Creative Direction

Create a futuristic, technological character while preserving the sound's impact and sense of immersion.

Sound Palette

Synthesizers create sci-fi weapons and other effects, while Foley and extensive processing build alien sonic textures.

Mix Approach

Panning simulates three-dimensional space, with the mix organized and processed in functional layers.
